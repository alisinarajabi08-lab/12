DOWNSTREAM

Delivering energy to end users through advanced processing and distribution systems.

Oil Refining & Processing

Noor Afroz provides comprehensive oil refining and processing services, converting crude oil into high-quality petroleum products in accordance with international standards and market requirements.

These services include:

  • Refinery Process Design: Developing efficient refining processes to maximize product yield and quality.
  • Process Optimization: Enhancing refinery performance through advanced technologies and operational improvements.
  • Quality Control and Compliance: Ensuring products meet regulatory and environmental standards.

Petroleum Products Distribution

Noor Afroz delivers integrated distribution solutions for refined petroleum products, ensuring reliable and efficient supply from refineries to end users.

These services include:

  • Supply Chain Management: Coordinating transportation, storage, and delivery of petroleum products.
  • Logistics and Scheduling: Optimizing distribution networks to ensure timely and cost-effective delivery.
  • Retail and Bulk Delivery Systems: Supporting both large-scale industrial clients and retail fuel networks.

Petrochemical Production

Noor Afroz supports petrochemical production by transforming hydrocarbon resources into valuable chemical and industrial products, contributing to diversified revenue streams.

These services include:

  • Process Design and Engineering: Developing petrochemical production units based on modern technologies.
  • Plant Operations Support: Assisting in efficient and safe plant operations.
  • Product Development and Quality Assurance: Ensuring consistent product quality and compliance with industry standards.
