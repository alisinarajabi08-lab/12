Strategic Partnership Between Noor Afroz and Pure Path Chemical Engineering Technology
Admin 27th Jul 2026 Technical Discussion, Management

As part of efforts to develop Afghanistan’s oil and gas industry and expand international cooperation, the leadership team of Noor Afroz held a meeting with the leadership delegation of Pure Path Chemical Engineering Technology. During the meeting, building on their successful cooperation in the past, both parties finalized and signed the framework for their formal partnership. Under this agreement, Noor Afroz will serve as the official representative of Pure Path Chemical Engineering Technology in Afghanistan, providing companies, investors, and stakeholders in the country’s oil and gas sector with access to advanced technologies, engineering services, and specialized consultancy. This partnership represents an important step toward the transfer of knowledge and technology, strengthening technical capacities, and developing oil and gas projects in Afghanistan in accordance with international standards.

Noor Afroz Expands Cooperation in Afghanistan’s Energy Sector
Admin 04th Jun 2026 Technical Discussion, Management

As part of efforts to expand economic and technical cooperation, the leadership and technical experts of Noor Afroz held meetings with the leadership and technical teams of Center For Monitoring New Technology and KR Group. During these meetings, opportunities for cooperation in oil services, energy, technology transfer, and technical expertise were discussed, and both sides agreed to strengthen their commercial and technical relations. Under the agreements reached, Noor Afroz will serve as the representative of these companies in Afghanistan, facilitating the introduction of their services and specialized solutions in the country. This cooperation represents an important step toward technology transfer, strengthening technical capacities, and implementing joint projects in Afghanistan’s energy sector.
