UPSTREAM

From exploration to drilling, we unlock energy resources efficiently.

Noor Afroz provides specialized exploration and subsurface evaluation services, aimed at optimizing exploration outcomes and improving the accuracy of development decisions.

Exploration & Subsurface Services

These services include:

  • Seismic Data Acquisition and Interpretation: Collecting and analyzing seismic data to understand subsurface structures and identify potential hydrocarbon zones.
  • Integrated Geological and Geophysical Studies: Conducting comprehensive studies that combine geological, geophysical, and geochemical data to create a holistic view of the reservoir.
  • Advanced Reservoir Modeling: Using sophisticated software and techniques to simulate reservoir behavior and predict performance, assisting in efficient reservoir development planning.

Drilling & Well Construction

Integrated drilling and well construction services are delivered with a strong emphasis on operational safety (HSE), efficiency, and compliance with international oil and gas standards. These services ensure high performance, well integrity, and operational reliability under complex reservoir and geological conditions.

These services include:

  • Application of Modern Drilling Technologies: Utilization of advanced drilling systems and equipment to enhance operational efficiency and drilling accuracy.
  • Optimized Engineering Practices: Implementation of engineered drilling programs and best practices to ensure safe and efficient well construction.
  • Precise Operational Management: Continuous supervision and control of drilling operations to maintain performance standards, safety compliance, and operational reliability.

Well Completion & Intervention

To optimize production and enhance hydrocarbon recovery, Noor Afroz provides specialized well completion and intervention services based on sound engineering principles and actual reservoir conditions.

These services include:

  • Completion Design and Execution: Designing and implementing well completion strategies tailored to reservoir characteristics to ensure optimal production performance.
  • Well Stimulation Operations: Applying techniques such as acidizing and hydraulic fracturing to enhance reservoir permeability and improve hydrocarbon flow.
  • Remedial Interventions: Conducting corrective operations to resolve well issues, improve efficiency, and extend the productive life of the well.

Upstream Project Management

Within the framework of an integrated upstream asset management approach, Noor Afroz provides comprehensive project management services across the entire project lifecycle, ensuring efficient and reliable delivery in line with oil and gas industry standards.

These services include:

  • Feasibility Studies: Evaluating technical and economic viability to support informed decision-making at early project stages.
  • Engineering Design: Developing detailed engineering solutions that meet project requirements and industry standards.
  • Project Execution: Managing and coordinating all operational activities to ensure projects are delivered on time and within budget.
  • Commissioning: Overseeing the safe and efficient startup of facilities and systems to ensure operational readiness.
  • Performance Monitoring: Continuously tracking project performance with a focus on time, cost, quality, and HSE compliance.
