Who We Are

Noor Afroz is an integrated energy company focused on oil, gas, LNG, and industrial infrastructure development across Afghanistan and the region.

Our Security

Noor Afroz maintains strict safety and security standards to ensure reliable operations, asset protection, and compliance across all energy projects.

Our Process

Noor Afroz follows a structured process from feasibility studies and engineering design to execution, commissioning, and operational support for energy projects.

Company history at a glance

Noor Afroz was established to support Afghanistan’s growing energy sector through integrated oil and gas solutions. The company focuses on upstream, midstream, downstream, EPC, and industrial infrastructure projects while promoting sustainable development, operational excellence, and long-term energy security across the region.
