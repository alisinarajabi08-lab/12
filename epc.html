EPC (Engineering, Procurement & Construction)

Turning Energy Vision into Reality Through Integrated EPC Solutions

Noor Afroz delivers comprehensive EPC (Engineering, Procurement & Construction) services for the oil, gas, energy, and industrial sectors. Through an integrated project delivery approach, we provide end-to-end solutions from conceptual design to commissioning, ensuring projects are executed safely, efficiently, on time, and in accordance with international standards.

Our EPC capabilities are designed to support complex energy infrastructure projects with a strong focus on technical excellence, operational reliability, cost optimization, and HSE compliance.

Engineering Services

Noor Afroz provides advanced engineering solutions tailored to the technical and operational requirements of energy projects.

These services include:

  • Conceptual & FEED Studies: Developing conceptual designs and Front-End Engineering Design (FEED) packages to support strategic decision-making and project planning.
  • Detailed Engineering Design: Delivering detailed multidisciplinary engineering solutions for mechanical, civil, electrical, instrumentation, and process systems.
  • Process Engineering: Designing efficient process systems that optimize production performance, energy efficiency, and operational reliability.
  • Technical Documentation & Compliance: Preparing technical specifications, drawings, procedures, and documentation in compliance with international engineering standards.
  • Value Engineering & Optimization: Improving project efficiency and reducing operational costs through optimized engineering solutions.

Procurement & Supply Chain Management

Noor Afroz manages procurement activities with a focus on quality assurance, cost efficiency, timely delivery, and supply chain reliability.

These services include:

  • Vendor & Supplier Management: Identifying, evaluating, and managing qualified international and local suppliers.
  • Equipment & Material Procurement: Supplying high-quality equipment, machinery, piping systems, valves, electrical components, and industrial materials.
  • Logistics & Transportation Coordination: Managing transportation, customs clearance, warehousing, and delivery operations.
  • Quality Assurance & Inspection: Conducting inspection and quality control procedures to ensure compliance with project specifications and standards.
  • Contract & Procurement Administration: Managing procurement contracts, documentation, and supplier performance.

Construction & Installation

Noor Afroz executes construction and installation projects with a commitment to safety, quality, efficiency, and operational excellence.

These services include:

  • Civil & Structural Construction: Executing foundations, structures, roads, industrial buildings, and infrastructure works.
  • Mechanical Installation: Installing pipelines, rotating equipment, pressure vessels, tanks, and processing systems.
  • Electrical & Instrumentation Works: Delivering electrical systems, substations, control systems, automation, and instrumentation installation.
  • Pipeline & Facility Construction: Building oil, gas, and energy infrastructure facilities in accordance with international HSE standards.
  • Site Supervision & Construction Management: Coordinating field activities to ensure schedule compliance, quality control, and operational safety.

Commissioning & Start-Up

Noor Afroz provides integrated commissioning and startup services to ensure facilities operate safely, efficiently, and according to design requirements.

These services include:

  • Pre-Commissioning Activities: Conducting system checks, testing, flushing, calibration, and readiness verification.
  • System Integration & Testing: Verifying functionality and operational performance of all systems and equipment.
  • Operational Readiness Support: Preparing operational teams, procedures, and documentation for facility startup.
  • Performance Testing & Optimization: Monitoring system performance and optimizing operational efficiency.
  • Handover & Project Closeout: Ensuring smooth transition from project execution to operational phase.

EPC Project Management

Noor Afroz delivers professional EPC project management services across the entire project lifecycle, ensuring integrated coordination and successful project execution.

These services include:

  • Project Planning & Scheduling: Developing execution plans, schedules, and resource allocation strategies.
  • Cost Control & Budget Management: Monitoring project costs and ensuring budget compliance.
  • Risk Management & HSE Compliance: Implementing risk mitigation strategies and maintaining strict HSE standards.
  • Quality Management Systems: Ensuring all project activities meet international quality standards.
  • Integrated Project Coordination: Managing communication and coordination between engineering, procurement, construction, and client teams.
